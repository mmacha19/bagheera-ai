SMS Program

Bagheera AI sends SMS messages — appointment reminders, confirmations, rescheduling notifications, and post-visit care follow-ups — to established patients of dental practices that subscribe to our platform. Currently piloting with Plymouth Dentistry.

Patients opt in through their dental practice's new-patient intake form (paper or digital) by affirmatively checking an SMS consent box. Full opt-in process, exact consent language, and intake form examples are documented on our SMS Consent page.

Sample message: "Plymouth Dentistry: You're confirmed for Thu May 14, 2:30 PM with Dr. Patel. Reply R to reschedule, STOP to unsubscribe, HELP for help."

Reply STOP to unsubscribe at any time. Reply HELP for help. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.
