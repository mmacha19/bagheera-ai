The Treatment Chaser.

One product One problem Built for dental teams

Every dental practice has dozens of patients walking out with unscheduled treatment plans worth thousands of dollars. Most never come back.

The Treatment Chaser is a focused, single-purpose platform that solves this one problem well. It connects to a practice’s existing practice management system (Dentrix, Eaglesoft, Open Dental), identifies patients with open treatment plans, and sends personalized, HIPAA-compliant SMS follow-ups on behalf of the practice — with full opt-out support, time-of-day gating, and an audit log for every message sent.

Current product · Treatment Chaser
Recover unscheduled treatment revenue.

Automated SMS follow-ups for patients with open, unaccepted treatment plans. Personalized per patient, time-windowed to TCPA-compliant hours, rate-limited per practice. Patient replies route to the front desk so a real human books the appointment.

02 /

Built on boring, audited infrastructure.

HIPAA-aware TCPA-aware CTIA / 10DLC Always opt-in

Bagheera AI handles protected health information (PHI) on behalf of dental practices, which means we operate under strict regulatory standards. Every design decision — from how we store data, to when we’re allowed to send messages, to how we honor opt-outs — is shaped by those standards.

HIPAA Compliance

Business Associate Agreement signed with every dental practice customer before any patient data flows. PHI encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. No PHI in logs.

TCPA-Aware Sending

SMS only goes to patients who have provided prior express written consent on the practice’s intake form. Sending strictly between 9 AM and 6 PM in the practice’s local timezone.

CTIA / 10DLC Compliant

Registered A2P 10DLC brand and campaign. STOP, HELP, and UNSUBSCRIBE keywords supported. Opt-outs honored permanently and immediately.

No Data Sharing

We never sell, share, rent, or trade patient mobile information, phone numbers, or PHI to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes, ever.
