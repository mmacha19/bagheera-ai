SMS Consent Process

Last Updated: May 7, 2026

Overview

Bagheera AI LLC ("Bagheera") sends SMS messages to dental patients on behalf of dental practices that use our platform. Patients opt in to receive SMS communications through the dental practice's standard patient intake process. This page documents the consent language and mechanisms used, in compliance with the Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA) and the CTIA Messaging Principles and Best Practices.

Consent is collected by the dental practice (Bagheera's customer) at the time the patient establishes care with the practice. Consent is never obtained from purchased lists, third-party data providers, or unsolicited contact lists.

How Patients Opt In

1. New-Patient Intake Form (in office)

When a patient establishes care with a dental practice that uses Bagheera, the patient completes a new-patient intake form. The intake form includes a dedicated SMS communications consent checkbox with the language shown below. The patient must affirmatively check the box and sign the form for SMS consent to be recorded; the box is unchecked by default.

2. Digital Intake (where supported by the practice)

Some practices collect new-patient intake digitally through their practice management software or patient portal. When SMS consent is collected digitally, the same consent language above is presented next to a checkbox that is unchecked by default. The patient must affirmatively check the box to opt in.

Exact Consent Language

The following consent language is used on every intake channel, on paper and digital:

"I agree to receive SMS appointment reminders, confirmations, treatment plan follow-ups, and care communications from [Practice Name], delivered through Bagheera AI. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe at any time. Reply HELP for help."

What SMS Consent Authorizes

By opting in, the patient agrees to receive the following types of SMS messages from Bagheera on behalf of their dental practice:

  • Appointment reminders and confirmations
  • Treatment plan follow-ups for previously discussed, unscheduled treatment
  • Insurance and benefits-related communications
  • Care-related messages from the practice (e.g., post-procedure check-ins)

SMS consent does not authorize, and Bagheera does not send:

  • Marketing or promotional messages from third parties
  • Messages to anyone who has not opted in through the practice
  • Messages to lead lists, purchased contact lists, or non-patients

How to Opt Out

Patients can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any SMS message they receive from Bagheera. Upon receiving STOP, Bagheera immediately suppresses the patient's number from all future SMS sends and confirms the unsubscribe with a final SMS. Patients may also reply HELP at any time, or contact us at mmacha1106@gmail.com, for assistance.

Industry-standard opt-out keywords (STOP, STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, QUIT) are all honored.

Time-of-Day Restrictions

Bagheera only sends non-emergency SMS messages between 9:00 AM and 8:00 PM in the recipient's local time, in compliance with TCPA quiet-hours requirements.

Data Sharing

Phone numbers collected through the intake process are not sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing purposes. Data sharing is limited to the service providers that operate the messaging service (such as Twilio) under strict confidentiality agreements. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
