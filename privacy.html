Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 7, 2026

Introduction

Bagheera AI LLC ("Bagheera," "we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect personal information when dental practices ("Practices") use our platform and when patients of those practices interact with communications sent through our service.

Who We Serve

Bagheera is a business-to-business platform that provides dental practices with software for treatment plan follow-ups, insurance verification, and AI-powered patient communications. We act as a service provider and Business Associate to the Practices that use our platform. Practices are responsible for their own privacy policies and patient relationships; this Policy describes our practices as a service provider.

Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Practice Account Information: Names, work email addresses, phone numbers, and roles of authorized practice users for account creation and authentication.
  • Patient Information (provided by Practices): Patient name, phone number, date of birth, treatment plan details, appointment data, and insurance information that Practices upload or sync from their practice management software for the sole purpose of operating the services they have engaged us to provide.
  • Communications Data: SMS messages, voice call transcripts, and responses exchanged through our platform on behalf of Practices.
  • Usage Data: How Practice users interact with the platform, including features used and actions taken.

Mobile Information and SMS Messaging

IMPORTANT: Bagheera sends SMS messages to patients on behalf of dental Practices for treatment plan reminders, appointment confirmations, and other patient care communications authorized by the Practice. We also send SMS messages to Practice users for account authentication.

SMS Program Details:

  • Sender Phone Number: SMS messages will be sent from a phone number assigned to Bagheera by our messaging provider. The sending number will appear on each message you receive.
  • Message Frequency: Patients will receive SMS messages as needed for treatment plan follow-ups and appointment-related communications authorized by their dental practice (typically no more than a few messages per month per patient). Practice users will receive SMS messages as needed for authentication purposes.
  • Message & Data Rates: Standard message and data rates may apply based on your mobile carrier's plan.

Opt-Out Instructions:

  • To Stop Messages: Reply STOP to any SMS message you receive from Bagheera at any time to opt-out. Once you opt out, you will not receive further SMS messages from Bagheera. Patients who opt out may need to contact their dental practice directly for treatment and appointment communications.
  • For Help: Reply HELP to any SMS message you receive from Bagheera, or email us at mmacha1106@gmail.com for assistance.

We will NEVER:

  • Sell your phone number to third parties
  • Share your phone number with lead generators
  • Use your phone number for marketing or promotional purposes beyond the patient-care communications authorized by your dental practice
  • Provide your phone number to advertisers or data brokers

Your phone number is used exclusively for:

  • Treatment plan reminders and appointment-related communications authorized by your dental practice
  • Account verification and authentication for Practice users
  • Security alerts related to your account
  • Essential service communications

By providing your phone number to a dental practice that uses Bagheera, you consent to receive SMS messages from Bagheera on behalf of that practice for the purposes described above.

How We Use Your Information

We use information to:

  • Provide and maintain our platform and the services Practices have engaged us to perform
  • Send treatment plan follow-ups, appointment reminders, and other patient communications on behalf of Practices
  • Verify insurance eligibility through authorized third-party providers
  • Operate AI-powered voice and messaging agents for inbound and outbound patient communications
  • Improve and personalize the platform experience for Practice users
  • Ensure the security of our platform
  • Comply with legal obligations, including HIPAA where applicable

HIPAA and Protected Health Information

Bagheera operates as a Business Associate under HIPAA when handling Protected Health Information (PHI) on behalf of Practices. We enter into a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with each Practice and require BAAs with subcontractors that handle PHI. PHI is encrypted in transit and at rest, access is logged and limited to authorized personnel, and patient identifiers are never used in logs or monitoring data.

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. Specifically, we do not and will never sell, rent, or share your mobile phone number with third parties, lead generators, marketers, or advertisers for promotional purposes.

We may share information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • With Your Dental Practice: Patient information, message responses, and engagement data are shared with the dental practice that authorized the communications, so they can manage your care.
  • Service Providers: We use third-party services that process data on our behalf under strict confidentiality and, where applicable, Business Associate Agreements. These include Supabase (data storage), Twilio (SMS delivery), Retell AI (voice agent), Zuub (insurance verification), Kolla Unify (practice management integrations), and Railway (hosting). These providers are only authorized to use your information as necessary to provide services to us, not for their own marketing purposes.
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law or to protect our rights and safety.
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, but only with the same privacy protections in place.

Your Privacy Controls

You have control over your information:

  • Opt Out of SMS: Reply STOP to any SMS message you receive from Bagheera at any time.
  • Access and Correction: Patients should contact their dental practice directly to access, correct, or delete patient records, as the practice is the custodian of that data. Practice users may update or delete their account information from within the platform.
  • Account Deletion: Practice users can request deletion of their account and associated data by contacting us.

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including encryption of sensitive information, secure data transmission over SSL/TLS, role-based access controls, audit logging, and regular security reviews. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

Data Retention

We retain information for as long as a Practice's account is active or as needed to provide services. If a Practice terminates its account, we will delete or return covered information in accordance with the applicable Business Associate Agreement, except where we are required to retain it for legal purposes.

Children's Privacy

Bagheera is not directed to children. Our platform is used by dental practices, which may communicate with patients of any age under the supervision of a parent or legal guardian as required by applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children except as authorized by a Practice in connection with the patient's care.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on our website and updating the "Last Updated" date.
