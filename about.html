About · Bagheera AI LLC

A small team building operations software for dental practices.

Bagheera AI LLC is a Florida healthcare technology company building the Treatment Chaser — a focused, HIPAA-aware platform that recovers unscheduled treatment revenue for independent dental practices.

01 /

The mission.

Independent Dental practices Single product Done right

Independent dental practices lose tens of thousands of dollars every year to unscheduled treatment. We’re building one product that fixes that, and we’re building it carefully.

Dental practices are some of the most operationally complex small businesses in healthcare. Their software is older than most of their staff. Their front desks are overwhelmed. The result: patients who could and should come back for care simply don’t, because nobody had the bandwidth to call them. Bagheera AI exists to close that gap — quietly, in the background, on rails the practice can audit.

Why “Bagheera”?

From Kipling Quiet Intelligent Protective

Bagheera is the black panther from Rudyard Kipling’s The Jungle Book — quiet, intelligent, and protective. We named the company after Bagheera because the best operational software runs in the background, never gets in the way, and quietly does the work that needs doing. That’s what we’re building for dental teams.
